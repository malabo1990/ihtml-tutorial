When your animation is ready, IHTML gives you several outputs. Pick one based on where it will be seen.

## For the web: Export HTML

**Export HTML** opens a dialog with two options:

- **Single HTML file**: one self-contained `.html` with the animation engine built in. The easiest to upload.
- **Separate HTML + CSS + JS**: three files, handy if you're adding it to your own site.

The result works in any browser and on any hosting, and also inside an `<iframe>` or a CMS. It animates exactly like it does in the editor.

## To send the project: Export (.ihtml)

**Export** downloads `project.ihtml` with the whole project: elements, animations, actions, fonts, images and video.

- Whoever receives it opens it with **IHTML Mirror**, the free app for desktop and Android.
- You open it again with **Import** to keep editing.

> An `.ihtml` file doesn't open by double-clicking it in a browser. If someone needs to see it without installing anything, use Export HTML.

## Image, GIF or video

**Export image / GIF / video** turns the artboard into a still image (PNG, WebP, JPEG), an animated GIF or a video (MP4, WebM). You choose the FPS and scale.

## See it on your phone

**Phone preview** shows a QR code: scan it with your phone to see the project live. It reloads on its own while you edit.

## What you learned

- Export HTML to publish on the web.
- Export `.ihtml` to share the editable project.
- Export an image, GIF or video, and see it on your phone.
